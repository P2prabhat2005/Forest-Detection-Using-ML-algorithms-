# Draft — Hellenic Fire Service data clarification (NOT SENT)

**To:** Hellenic Fire Service, incident-records/open-data team  
**Subject:** Clarification of wildfire incident dates and records for Greece–BC research

Dear Hellenic Fire Service team,

We are assessing whether your public agroforestry incident records can support a daily, area-based research target: **a newly recorded, agency-confirmed, unplanned wildland fire on its recorded discovery day**, regardless of eventual burned area. We are comparing definitions with British Columbia; we are not requesting a bulk extract. Could you please clarify the following for the published incident workbooks (including 2024–2025)?

1. What exactly do `Ημερ/νία Έναρξης` and `Ώρα Έναρξης` mean: estimated physical ignition, first discovery, first report/call, dispatch, or incident opening? Are they revised later? If they are not discovery, is a recorded first-discovery date available for the same incidents? Which local reporting calendar, time zone and daylight-saving convention govern the fields, particularly near midnight?
2. Which records represent confirmed, unplanned **wildland** fires? Please provide field definitions or codes for excluding false alarms, smoke investigations, planned/prescribed burns, agricultural-only or structural fires, and duplicates. How is an escaped prescribed burn recorded? Is every published row a confirmed incident?
3. What do `X-ENGAGE` and `Y-ENGAGE` locate—origin/discovery point, dispatch/response point, or another location? What are their coordinate reference system and typical accuracy? How should absent, rounded, or implausible coordinates be interpreted?
4. Which identifier uniquely tracks one incident across corrections? How are repeat reports, merged/complex fires, cross-jurisdiction incidents and reactivations handled? Does the public file retain corrected records or only the latest version?
5. What geography, incident types and dates are completely covered by the public series? Are some jurisdictions or years incomplete? When is a reporting day/year considered final, and can records be added, reclassified or backdated afterward?

A versioned data dictionary, classification code list, coverage note and a few de-identified examples of ambiguous records would be sufficient. We would appreciate a contact for follow-up if another team owns these definitions.

Kind regards,  
Forest Fire Early-Warning research team

**Public source referenced:** [Hellenic Fire Service open-data catalogue](https://www.fireservice.gr/el/synola-dedomenon).
